Overview

International Bridge is a multiplayer card game. This policy explains what information the app uses to create rooms, connect players, support calls, and keep games running.

Information used by the app

  • Player-entered display names
  • Room and seating information needed to join and play games
  • Game state and scoring data required to run live matches
  • Camera and microphone access only when you choose to use in-game calling

Camera and microphone

Camera and microphone access are used only for the live call feature. If you do not join a call, the app does not need those permissions for normal gameplay.

Tracking and advertising

International Bridge does not use third-party advertising and does not use app tracking for cross-app or cross-site advertising purposes.

Data sharing

The app shares gameplay and room information only as needed among connected players in the same game session so the multiplayer experience can function correctly.
